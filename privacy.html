Website information

Privacy, handled with care.

This is a starter policy for the current website template. It must be reviewed and updated to match the services, providers and laws that apply before publication.

Template notice: This draft is not legal advice. It currently assumes a mostly static informational site and a contact form that opens the visitor's email app. Update it if analytics, cookies, a hosted form, newsletter, accounts or other third-party services are added.

1. Information you provide

If you choose to contact us through your email application, the information you send is handled by your email provider and the recipient mailbox you choose. The current website form does not itself store submissions in a website database.

2. Technical information

The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address, browser details, request timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the site. Confirm the final host's practices and documentation before launch.

3. Purpose and use

Information voluntarily sent to Neonaire may be used to respond to the enquiry, assess a proposed collaboration or handle related communications. Do not send sensitive personal information through a general enquiry form.

4. Sharing and service providers

Website hosting, email and any future form, analytics or anti-spam providers may process information as required to provide their services. Name the actual providers and explain their role once selected.

5. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose or applicable legal requirements. Appropriate safeguards should be selected for the real systems used; no internet transmission or storage method is completely risk-free.

6. Your choices and requests

Provide a verified contact address for privacy questions and requests before publishing this policy. Applicable rights and procedures depend on the laws relevant to the operator and visitor.

7. Children

This site is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If the website's audience or services change, review the relevant age and consent requirements.

8. Updates and contact

Update this page whenever the website's data practices change. Add the effective date, legal operator name, and verified privacy contact before launch.
