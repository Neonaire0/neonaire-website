How we approach a new venture

Start small.
Prove what matters.

Each opportunity should move through a disciplined process: understand the need, validate the idea, build a prototype, learn from users and scale only when the fundamentals are sound.

01 · Discover

Understand the problem, audience and context.

02 · Validate

Test assumptions and evaluate feasibility.

03 · Build

Create a focused, usable first version.

04 · Improve

Learn, measure and grow responsibly.
